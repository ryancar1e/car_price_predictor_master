How the ML Model Works

End-to-End Pipeline Formulation based on rajtilakls2510/car_price_predictor

1
Data Ingestion
Ingests 892 raw automotive listings from Quikr with 6 attributes including price, year, and kms.
2
Data Cleaning
Strips 'kms' units, removes non-numeric 'Ask for Price' rows, slices names to 3 words, and prunes luxury outliers.
3
ColumnTransformer
One-hot encodes nominal columns ('name', 'company', 'fuel_type') while passing numerical attributes.
4
Linear Regression
Fits Ordinary Least Squares regression, achieving an R² determination score of ~0.92 on test splits.
5
Atomic Inference
Serialized pipeline delivers instantaneous prediction directly to this interactive web interface.
